# Staff guide: editing the CRTDH website

This guide is for lab staff who keep the website up to date. You don't need any technical knowledge.

Words used in this guide:

- **CMS**: the private editing website. Ask your admin for its address. It starts with `https://cms.`
- **Live site**: the public website, https://crtdh.iitkgp.ac.in/
- **Editor**: you can make changes and ask for a review.
- **Publisher**: you can also put changes on the live site, and bring back old versions.
- **Admin**: you can also add people and reset their sign-in.

## The one rule: nothing is live until it is published

Every change you make is saved straight away, but only in the CMS. The live site stays the same. Your change
appears there only when a Publisher clicks **Publish**.

That's what **"Saved · not live yet"** means. Your work is safe, and visitors can't see it yet.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the CMS address.
2. First you see a Cloudflare page. Type your email address. A code arrives by email. Type it in.
3. Now you see the CRTDH sign-in. Type your **Username** and **Password**.

**The first time only**

- Type the temporary password you were given. Then choose your own: **at least 12 characters**. A short
  sentence works well.

**If it goes wrong**

- "Sign-in is paused": too many wrong tries. Wait a few minutes.
- Forgot your password: ask an Admin to reset it. You get a temporary password and choose a new one.

To sign out, click your name at the top, then **Sign out**. If you used a shared computer, choose
**Sign out on all devices**.

---

## 2. Find your way around

- **Left:** the list of **Pages**, the same pages as the website (Home, About, Team, Media …). A dot next to a page
  means it has changes that are not live yet. When you open a page, its **sections** appear under it, numbered.
- **Middle:** the real page, as visitors will see it.
- **Right:** the fields of the part you picked.
- **Bar at the top:** how many changes are not live yet, plus **Preview** and **Request review** (or
  **Review & publish** for Publishers).

Lower on the left: **Review & publish** and **Publish history**. To change the contact details, logos or menu
links that appear on every page, click the header or the footer on any page (or **Header & footer** on the left,
if you see it). Publishers also see **Messages** (from the contact form). Admins also see **Users**.

---

## 3. Change some text

1. Open the page on the left, for example **Home**.
2. Click the part you want to change, right on the page. Or click its number in the section list.
3. Change the words in the fields on the right.
4. Stop typing. After a second you see **Saving…**, then **Saved · not live yet**.

There is no Save button. You don't need one.

**What the messages mean**

| You see | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Saved · not live yet | Saved in the CMS. Not on the live site. | Nothing. |
| Offline · kept on this computer | Your internet dropped. Your text is kept. | Wait. It saves when you are back online. |
| Not saved · check the field | Something in a field is not allowed (too long, missing). | Read the red message under the field and fix it. |
| Not saved · changed by someone else | A colleague changed this at the same time. | Click **Reload the page**. Use **Copy my text** first if you want to keep your words. |

Text formatting: you can use **Bold**, *Italic*, links, bulleted and numbered lists, and a **Small heading**.
Nothing else, so every page looks the same.

---

## 4. Change the big photo on the Home page (the hero)

1. Open **Home** and click the big photo at the top (section **Hero**).
2. On the right, pick the slide you want to change.
3. Under **Photo**, click **Replace photo** or drop a photo onto the box.
4. Click **Save photo** (or **Cancel** to keep the old one). That's all: the photo is centred automatically.

**Photo size rules**

- Home page big photos must be **at least 1600 pixels wide**. A smaller photo is refused, and the old photo stays.
- Other photos should be at least 1200 pixels wide. A smaller one is allowed, but you get a warning that it may
  look blurry.
- Use the original photo from the camera or phone. Photos sent through WhatsApp are made smaller and are usually
  too small.
- JPEG, PNG or WebP. Up to 15 MB.
- Not sure what to photograph? Open **Photo shot list** in the CMS (link **See the shot list** next to the photo).

---

## 5. Add a person to the Team page

1. Open **Team** → section **Team members**.
2. Click **Add team member**.
3. Fill in **Name**, **Group** (for example Faculty), **Designation**, and if you like **Institution** and **Short bio**.
4. Add a **Photo**: a portrait, chest up, against a plain wall. It is shown upright (4:5). Fill in the **Photo
   description**.
5. **Turn on "Shown on site".** New people start hidden, so nobody appears by accident.
6. To change the order, drag the person in the list. With the keyboard: press Space, use the arrow keys, then
   press Space again.

---

## 6. Add an event or camp to the Media page

1. Open **Media** → section **Events and camps**.
2. Click **Add event**.
3. Fill in **Title**, **Date**, **Type** (Health camp, Visit, Workshop, Event) and **Description**.
4. Under **Photos**, click **Add first photo**. Choose a photo and add a
   **Caption** if you like. Add more photos the same way.
5. **Turn on "Shown on site"** for the event.

News articles go in **Media coverage** (**Add article**: **Headline** and **Article link**).

---

## 7. Hide something, or remove it

- **Hide** (anyone): open the item and turn off **Shown on site**. It disappears from the site after the next
  publish, but stays in the CMS. Turn it back on any time.
- **Delete** (Publishers only): **Delete …** at the bottom of the item. It stays on the live site until the next
  publish, and you can still bring it back by discarding that change on **Review & publish**. Most of the time,
  hiding is the better choice.

---

## 8. Check your work: Preview

Click **Preview** in the top bar. You see the page as it will look once published, with all unpublished changes.
Use the **Desktop** and **Phone** buttons to check both sizes. Preview is private: only signed-in CMS users can
see it.

---

## 9. Put your changes live

**If you are an Editor: ask for a review**

1. Click **Request review** in the top bar.
2. Optionally, write a **Note for the publisher**, for example why the change is needed or when it should go live.
3. Click **Send request**. The bar shows **Waiting for review**. The Publishers get an email.

**If you are a Publisher: review and publish**

1. Open **Review & publish**. You see every change not yet live, grouped by page, with who made it and the old
   and new value.
2. Check each change. To keep one off the site, **Discard** it (see section 10).
3. Click **Publish N changes**. The whole site is updated at once, which takes about a minute.
4. Watch the status. It may say **waiting for upload machine** while the upload PC on campus picks it up, then
   **Live at 14:32**.
5. If it says **Publish failed**, read the reason. **The site is unchanged.** Click **Try again**, or tell the
   website admin.

If it keeps waiting for the upload machine, the campus upload PC is probably switched off. Ask someone to switch
it on.

---

## 10. Undo a change before it goes live: Discard

1. Open **Review & publish**.
2. Next to the change, choose **Discard change**, then confirm.
3. The item goes back to exactly what the live site shows now. If it was a new item, it is removed.

Discarding can't be undone. Your other changes stay.

---

## 11. See history, and bring back an old version

- **One item's history:** open the item and click **Edit history** at the bottom of the fields. You see who
  changed it, when, and whether it was done in the CMS or by Claude.
- **The whole site (Publishers):** open **Publish history**. Every publish is kept as a numbered version. Click
  **Restore** next to a version and confirm. Its content comes back as **unpublished changes**. Check them in
  Preview, then publish. Until then, the live site doesn't change.

---

## 12. Ask Claude to make changes

You can ask Claude (claude.ai) in plain English, for example "Change Prof. Sharma's designation to Professor and
Head". Your admin sets this up once. The steps are in **[Connect Claude to the CRTDH website CMS](connect-claude.md)**.

Good to know:

- Claude asks before each change. It saves changes as **unpublished**, exactly like you do. It can't publish,
  delete, or change user accounts.
- Claude gives you a preview link after each change. Open it to check.
- Need to add photos? Ask Claude for an **upload link**, then open it on your phone or computer.
- Changed your mind? Say "undo that" or "undo everything you did in this chat".
- On **Review & publish**, Claude's changes are marked, for example "Claude made 3 changes for Priya in one
  session". A Publisher can **Discard all from this Claude session** in one click.
- One chat can make up to 25 changes.

---

## 13. Quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I changed something but the website looks the same. | Normal. It is **not live yet**. A Publisher must publish it. |
| Can I break the website? | No. Nothing reaches the site without a publish, and a Publisher can put back any old version. |
| Someone changed the same thing as me. | You see **Not saved · changed by someone else**. Copy your text, reload, and try again. |
| My photo was refused. | It is too small. Use the original photo, at least 1600 px wide for the Home page. |
| I forgot my password. | Ask an Admin to reset it. You get a new temporary password. |
| Who do I ask for help? | Your website admin. If you see a code like a request id, send it to them. |
